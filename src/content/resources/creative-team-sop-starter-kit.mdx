**WHO THIS IS FOR**

Creative leads and studio owners running a team of more than one person, who are tired of quality and process depending on who happens to be handling a project that day.

**HOW TO USE IT**

Each SOP below is a starting template. Adapt the specifics to your team's actual tools and terminology, then treat it as the default way things are done — not a suggestion — unless someone has a good reason to deviate.

## SOP 1 — NEW PROJECT INTAKE

Purpose: make sure no project starts without the information the team needs to do it right.

- **1. Client brief or discovery questionnaire completed and reviewed by the project lead.**
- **2. Scope, budget, and timeline confirmed in writing before any design work begins.**
- **3. Project added to the team tracker with a deadline and a named owner.**
- **4. Kickoff note shared with everyone touching the project, summarizing the objective and constraints.**

## SOP 2 — DESIGN REVIEW

Purpose: catch quality and brand issues before work ever reaches the client.

- **1. Creator self-reviews against the original brief before submitting for review.**
- **2. A second team member — not the creator — reviews for brand consistency and craft.**
- **3. Feedback is given in writing and tied to the brief, not personal taste.**
- **4. Creator revises and resubmits; the reviewer confirms before the client sees it.**

## SOP 3 — CLIENT REVISION HANDLING

Purpose: keep revisions organized and protect the project's scope.

- **1. All revision requests are logged in one place, even ones received by phone or voice note.**
- **2. Each request is checked against the number of revision rounds actually agreed.**
- **3. Anything beyond scope is flagged to the client before any work begins on it.**
- **4. Each round of revisions is delivered as one complete update, not partial fixes trickling out.**

## SOP 4 — FILE HANDOFF & ARCHIVING

Purpose: make sure finished work stays usable and findable long after the project ends.

- **1. Files renamed and organized using the team's standard naming convention.**
- **2. All formats required by the brief are delivered — source, print-ready, and web, as needed.**
- **3. Final files archived in shared team storage, not left only on the creator's device.**
- **4. Project marked complete in the tracker, with final files linked.**

**MAKE IT YOURS**

Swap in your team's actual tool names — Trello, Notion, a shared drive, a WhatsApp group — wherever this kit says "tracker" or "shared storage." An SOP only works if it matches how your team actually communicates today, not an idealized version of it.

## NEXT STEPS

**These four SOPs cover the points where creative teams most often lose consistency: intake, review, revisions, and handoff. Add to this kit as you find your own team's repeated points of confusion — the pattern is always the same: write down what "good" looks like once, so it stops depending on memory.**
