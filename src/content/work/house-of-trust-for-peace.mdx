Every institution has a defining moment — its first impression on the world.
For the House of Trust for Peace (HOTP), that moment came with the launch of
its inaugural Practitioner Training Program in Buea, Cameroon, bringing
together peace practitioners, trainers, academic institutions, and strategic
partners around a shared vision: fostering trust as a foundation for
sustainable peace.

To support this milestone, HOTP needed more than a series of design assets —
they needed a complete visual communication system that could express
credibility, professionalism, and purpose across every participant
interaction. Sigma Studio was engaged to design that experience, in
partnership with UCAC, ACADIR, Catharsis, The Okwelians, RENOSCAM, and
Royalty World.

## Design principles

Every decision throughout the project was guided by five core principles:

1. **Clarity** — information should be easy to understand.
2. **Consistency** — every asset should feel like part of one system.
3. **Credibility** — visuals should inspire confidence and professionalism.
4. **Accessibility** — bilingual communication should feel balanced and inclusive.
5. **Longevity** — the identity should remain relevant beyond a single event.

## Outcome

- A scalable institutional visual identity.
- Cohesive participant experiences across multiple touchpoints.
- Professional communication materials for both physical and digital platforms.
- Production-ready assets suitable for large-scale implementation.
- A visual foundation designed to grow alongside the organization.

*Design has the power to shape how institutions are perceived. For the House
of Trust for Peace, this project was an opportunity to demonstrate how
thoughtful visual communication can strengthen credibility, improve
participant experience, and support meaningful social initiatives.*
