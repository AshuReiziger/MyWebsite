Orbit Interiors set out to become Cameroon's answer to what contemporary
interior design could be — local craftsmanship read through a modern,
premium lens. That ambition needed a brand strong enough to carry it.

## The Identity

The logo translates the strategy directly: an orbit-inspired symbol built
from intertwined loops, communicating movement, space, innovation, and
connection. "Orbit" sits in a bold, modern serif; "Interiors" in a lighter,
more delicate weight beside it — a pairing that reads as both confident and
personal.

The visual system built around that mark combines deep, sophisticated tones
with warm brown and subtle gold accents; elegant typography (Crake Test
paired with Montserrat); minimal, uncluttered layouts; and intentional
photography that showcases the spaces and craftsmanship behind the brand.

## The Brand System

Beyond the logo, the identity became a full system — guidelines covering
logo usage, color, typography, pattern, photography, and application —
built so the brand could scale consistently across every touchpoint:
stationery, apparel, signage, and outdoor advertising, all carrying the
same confident, premium tone.

*COME ALIVE. Making spaces and individuals come alive.*
