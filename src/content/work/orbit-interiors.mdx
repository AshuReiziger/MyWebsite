    heading: "Building the Strategic Foundation"
    body: "I began by defining the brand's purpose, audience, positioning, personality, and creative direction. The strategy centered on creating personalized, functional spaces that reflect the identity of the people who inhabit them. This led to the brand's central idea: COME ALIVE — making spaces and individuals come alive."
